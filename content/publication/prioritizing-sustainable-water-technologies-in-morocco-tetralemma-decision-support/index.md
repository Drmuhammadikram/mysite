abstract: "This study develops a tetralemma decision support framework for prioritizing sustainable water technologies in Morocco under uncertainty. Seven technologies were assessed against criteria spanning water security, equity, sustainability, and governance. An integrated fuzzy Trial and Evaluation Laboratory (DEMATEL), Analytic Network Process (ANP) and Technique for Order of Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution (TOPSIS) approach modeled experts' judgements and interdependencies. Results reveal that governance received the greatest weight (28.5%), followed by equity (25%), security (24.4%), and sustainability (22.1%). Anaerobic Membrane Bioreactor (AnMBR, T3) ranked highest, reflecting strong performance in technical, socio-economic, and environmental aspects. The framework supports transparent comparison of technology alternatives and guiding strategic investments and policy decisions toward sustainable water futures."
