Muhammad Ikram is a professor at the School of Business Administration,
Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane, Morocco. His work examines how formal
management systems — particularly ISO 14001 — translate into measurable
environmental performance, and how organizations in emerging economies
navigate the transition to low-carbon operations.

His research has appeared in *Journal of Cleaner Production*,
*Business Strategy and the Environment*, *Sustainable Production and
Consumption*, and *Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental
Management*, and has been cited more than 7,000 times.
