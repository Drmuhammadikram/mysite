Homicide is the only crime statistic comparable across countries — a death is recorded whatever the reporting culture, whereas theft, assault and sexual violence figures track willingness to report and police recording practice at least as much as underlying incidence. That is why no general crime rate appears here: recorded crime is not comparable between countries, and a map of it would produce confident colour where no comparison exists. UNODC reports with a two to three year lag, so the most recent years are thinly covered. Prison population has no common reporting year at all — each country is updated whenever its national statistics appear — so that view shows each country's most recent reading, with the year given on hover. It measures state action rather than criminality, and the two are less related than intuition suggests.
