Undernourishment estimates the share of the population whose habitual food consumption is insufficient for a normal active life; it is modelled from food supply and distribution data rather than measured directly. Severe food insecurity comes from the Food Insecurity Experience Scale, which asks people whether they have run out of food or gone a whole day without eating. The cost of a healthy diet is the least expensive combination of local foods meeting dietary guidelines, in purchasing-power-adjusted dollars per person per day — set it against the $3 a day extreme poverty line. Calorie supply measures what is available in the food system, not what people eat, and takes no account of distribution.
