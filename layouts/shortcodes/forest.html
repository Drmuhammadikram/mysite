Forest cover and net change come from FAO country reporting; tree cover loss is satellite-observed and counts all loss including harvest and fire, so it is larger than deforestation. Net change can be positive while deforestation continues, if planting exceeds clearance. Deforestation is reported only in five-year assessment periods and tree cover loss only from 2005, so where a year has no observation the map shows the most recent earlier one and the hover panel gives its date.
