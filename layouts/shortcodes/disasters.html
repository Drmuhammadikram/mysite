Natural hazard events recorded {{ .period }}, aggregated by country. Source: {{ .source }}. EM-DAT records events meeting at least one threshold: ten or more deaths, a hundred or more people affected, a declaration of emergency, or a call for international assistance. Reporting quality varies between countries and hazard types, so differences partly reflect monitoring capacity rather than exposure alone. People affected is generally a better guide to impact than event counts, which favour large and well-monitored countries.
