Air Quality Index
Live readings from monitoring stations worldwide.
Click a station

Station readings from the World Air Quality Index project, covering more than 12,000 stations in over 100 countries. The index is based on particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulphur dioxide and carbon monoxide. The modelled background layer draws on the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service via Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), and covers regions with no ground monitoring. Where stations exist, they measure; the model estimates.
